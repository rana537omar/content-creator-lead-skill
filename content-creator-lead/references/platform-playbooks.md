# Platform playbooks

Use this to adapt content to a specific platform, set up a profile, decide posting rhythm, or handle hashtags and keywords.

Platforms change features, limits and ranking signals often. Treat everything here as durable guidance, and confirm current specifics (maximum lengths, aspect ratios, monetization thresholds, new formats) in the platform's own help pages or with a web search when precision matters.

## 1. What every feed rewards

Recommendation systems try to predict whether a person will be glad they saw a post. The signals differ in name, not in kind:

- **Attention**: did people stop, and how long did they stay (watch time, completion, rewatches, dwell time)?
- **Active response**: shares and sends, saves, comments and replies, follows after viewing.
- **Satisfaction**: no quick swipe away, no "not interested", return visits.
- **Relevance**: a clear topic the system can match to people who like that topic.

So the cross-platform recipe is stable: a clear niche, a strong opening, content worth finishing, and something worth sharing or saving. Shares and saves are usually the strongest signs of value.

## 2. Instagram

- **Formats**: Reels for reach to new people; carousels for teaching, saves and shares; Stories for daily connection with existing followers, polls, questions and links; single images for announcements and quotes; Lives and broadcast channels for depth.
- **What works**: short Reels with an immediate hook and on-screen text; carousels with a strong first slide; content people send to friends.
- **Profile**: searchable name field with a keyword, a bio that states who it is for and what they get, one link, highlights organised by topic or offer, three pinned posts that represent the account.
- **Notes**: original content is favoured over reposts with other apps' watermarks. Stories and direct messages drive relationship and sales. Use the paid partnership label for sponsored content.

## 3. TikTok

- **Formats**: short vertical video first; photo carousels; longer videos where retention holds; Lives.
- **What works**: a hook in the first second, native and unpolished feel, fast pacing, trends and sounds adapted to the niche, replying to comments with videos, series ("part 2").
- **Search matters**: many people use it as a search engine. Say the key phrase aloud, put it in on-screen text and in the caption.
- **Notes**: each video is judged largely on its own, so small accounts can reach large audiences. Post more often and test more variations here than elsewhere.

## 4. YouTube (long form and Shorts)

- **Long form** is driven by click-through rate on title and thumbnail, and by watch time and satisfaction once clicked. It is also a search engine: videos can earn views for years.
- **What works**: a clear promise in the package, a hook that confirms it, strong structure and pacing, series and playlists, end screens pointing to the next video.
- **Shorts** bring discovery and subscribers; use them to introduce the channel's topics and point to long videos.
- **Metadata**: title with the main phrase, a description whose first two lines summarise the video and include key terms, chapters, relevant playlists. Tags matter little.
- **Notes**: consistency of topic helps the system learn who the channel is for. Study click-through rate and the retention graph for every video. Community posts keep subscribers warm between uploads.

## 5. LinkedIn

- **Formats**: text posts, document carousels (PDF), short native video, newsletters and articles, comments on others' posts.
- **What works**: specific professional insight from real experience, stories with a lesson, clear opinions, useful frameworks, a first line that earns "see more". Thoughtful comments on others' posts are a growth channel in themselves.
- **Profile**: headline that says who is helped and how, a banner that reinforces it, a featured section with the lead magnet or best work, an About section written to the reader.
- **Notes**: the audience skews to decision-makers, so a small following can be commercially valuable. Avoid engagement bait and mass tagging. Reply to comments early.

## 6. X and Threads

- **Formats**: short posts, threads, images, short video, replies and quote posts.
- **What works**: sharp single ideas, timely takes, threads that teach, conversation. Replying well to larger accounts in the niche brings visibility.
- **Notes**: volume and speed matter more here than elsewhere; several posts a day is normal. Test ideas here cheaply, then expand winners into longer content.

## 7. Facebook

- **Formats**: Reels and video, groups, pages, Lives, events.
- **What works**: shareable, relatable and community content; Reels for reach; groups for depth with a defined community; local and older audiences are strong here.
- **Notes**: repurposed vertical video performs well when exported clean. Groups need active moderation and regular prompts.

## 8. Pinterest

- **A visual search engine**, not a social feed. Content is discovered by keyword for months or years.
- **What works**: vertical pins with clear text overlay, keyword-rich titles and descriptions, boards organised by topic, fresh pins linking to the creator's site. Strong for food, home, fashion, beauty, crafts, weddings, travel and planning-led topics.

## 9. Podcast and newsletter

- **Podcast**: growth comes mostly from outside the podcast apps: clips on short video, guest appearances, cross-promotion, and search-friendly episode titles. Consistent schedule, a strong cold open, and clear episode titles that promise a benefit.
- **Newsletter**: growth comes from a clear promise, a lead magnet, mentions in every piece of social content, and recommendations from other newsletters. Health is measured by open and click rates and replies, and by keeping the list clean.

## 10. Profile setup checklist

- Handle: the same or close across platforms, easy to say and spell.
- Photo: a clear face for a personal brand, a clean logo for a company.
- Name field: includes what they do, for search.
- Bio: who it is for, what they get, a proof point, a call to action.
- Link: one destination that matches the current goal (lead magnet, booking, shop).
- Pinned content: best introduction, best proof, current offer.
- Contact options and category set correctly; a professional or creator account so analytics are available.
- Accessibility: captions on video, alt text on images.

Write bios as two or three options within the platform's character limit.

## 11. Hashtags and keywords

Platforms now understand topic mainly from the content itself: the words spoken, on-screen text, caption and the audience that engages. So:

- **Keywords first**: use the phrases the audience searches for in the spoken hook, on-screen text, caption, title and description, in natural wording.
- **Hashtags are a light supporting signal**: a few (often three to five) specific, relevant ones are enough. Mix a niche tag, a topic tag and, where relevant, a location or community tag.
- Avoid banned, irrelevant or giant generic tags, and avoid pasting the same block every time.
- A branded hashtag is useful for collecting community or campaign content.

## 12. Timing and frequency

- **Best time is account-specific.** Use the account's own analytics for when followers are active; test two or three slots for a few weeks and compare like with like. Generic "best time" tables are weak guides.
- **Frequency**: quality and consistency over volume. A sensible starting range: short video platforms three to seven a week; LinkedIn two to five a week; X and Threads daily; long YouTube video weekly or fortnightly; newsletter weekly or fortnightly. Fit this to capacity (see `strategy-and-positioning.md`).
- A post's results come from the content far more than from the clock.

## 13. Community and engagement

Growth is easier when the existing audience feels heard.

- Reply to comments, especially in the first hours and on newer accounts. Ask follow-up questions.
- Turn good comments and questions into new content, and credit the person.
- Spend a set time each day engaging with others in the niche: genuine, specific comments.
- Collaborate: joint posts, guest appearances, duets and stitches, shared lives. Pick partners with a similar audience and size.
- Set simple moderation rules: hide spam, remove abuse, do not argue with bad-faith comments, answer fair criticism calmly.
- Write a set of saved replies for common questions, in the creator's voice, and personalise them.

Never use bots, purchased engagement or pods. They damage reach, distort analytics and put the account at risk.
