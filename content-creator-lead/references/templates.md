## 1. Intake questionnaire

Send only the questions that are still unanswered.

1. What do you make content about, and what do you sell or want to be known for?
2. Who is it for? Describe one ideal follower or customer.
3. What is the main goal for the next three months (followers, leads, sales, authority, sponsorships)?
4. Which platforms are you on, and how many followers on each?
5. What formats are you comfortable with (on camera, voiceover, writing, design)?
6. How many hours a week can you give this? Do you have help or a budget?
7. Which three posts did best, and which flopped?
8. Which creators or brands do you admire, and what do you like about them?
9. How would you describe your tone? Anything you would never say or do?
10. What language and dialect should the content be in?

## 10. Performance tracker

One row per post. This layout works directly with `scripts/analyze_content.py`.

| date | platform | format | pillar | title | views | likes | comments | shares | saves | clicks | follows | retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Log each post at the same age (for example 7 days after posting). Use reach or impressions in the views column for posts that are not video. Retention is the average percentage viewed.
